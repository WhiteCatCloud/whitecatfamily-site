Delete Your Account

WhiteCat: Parental Controls — published by White Cat Cloud Inc.

The short version

You can delete your WhiteCat account yourself, from inside the app, in about ten seconds. It removes your account and your family's data from our systems. It cannot be undone.

Delete it from the app

  1. Open the WhiteCat app and sign in.
  2. Go to the Account tab.
  3. Tap your profile card at the top.
  4. Under ACCOUNT, tap Delete Account.
  5. Confirm twice. The second confirmation is final.

Your account is deleted immediately. You do not need to contact us, and there is nothing to wait for.

No longer have the app?

Email sales@whitecatcloud.com from the address on your account, with the subject “Delete my account”. We will verify it is you and complete the deletion within 30 days — usually much sooner.

What gets deleted, and what we keep

DataWhat happens
Your account and sign-inDeleted. Removed from our database and from our authentication provider.
Your name and email addressDeleted.
Family members and their profilesDeleted.
Devices on your networkDeleted, including device names and network identifiers.
Rules, schedules and policiesDeleted.
Internet activity and screen-time dataDeleted.
Your household recordAnonymised and deactivated — the name is replaced with [deleted].
Your routerDecommissioned and unlinked from your account. The hardware stays yours and can be set up again from scratch.
Support messages you sent usKept only as long as needed to resolve the issue, then deleted.
A record that a deletion happenedKept. A single audit entry, with no personal details, so we can prove the deletion was carried out.
Payment and billing recordsHeld by Apple or Google, not by us. Manage or cancel your subscription in the App Store or Google Play. Where tax law requires us to keep an invoice record, we keep it for the statutory period.

If your family has more than one parent

If you are the only owner of a household that still has other members in it, the app will ask you to remove those members first. This stops one person accidentally deleting a family that other people are still using.

Cancelling your subscription

Deleting your account does not automatically cancel an active subscription, because the subscription is held by Apple or Google. Cancel it in App Store › Subscriptions or Google Play › Subscriptions before or after deleting your account.
