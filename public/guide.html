Set up your WhiteCat

Five steps, about 10 minutes. You don't need to know anything about networks.

Before you start: keep your existing internet box (router or fibre modem) plugged in. WhiteCat sits behind it — it doesn't replace it.
1

Plug it in

In the box: the WhiteCat router, a power adapter and an Ethernet cable.

Run the Ethernet cable from your existing internet box into the port marked WAN on the back of the WhiteCat. Then plug in the power.

Give it about a minute. When the status light starts alternating green and red, it's booted and ready to pair.

2

Get the app

Everything is set up from the app. It's free.

Download on the App Store
GET IT ON Google Play

Reading this on a computer? Scan with your phone's camera — it opens the right store automatically.

3

Create your account

Open the app and tap Continue with Google or Continue with Apple. There's no separate password to invent.

Use the account you want the household's receipts and alerts to go to. You can invite a second parent later.

4

Pair the router

The app walks you through this. It asks your phone to join a temporary Wi-Fi network called WhiteCat-Setup for about 30 seconds, exchanges a key with the router, and switches you back.

You'll then choose your Network name (SSID) and Password — this is the Wi-Fi your family will use from now on.

When pairing finishes the status light turns solid blue. That's the router settled and healthy.

Pick a name you'll recognise. You won't have to type the password on every device — the next step shows you the QR shortcut.
5

Move your devices across

Connect your family's devices to the new WhiteCat Wi-Fi — tablets, TVs, laptops, consoles, phones. Anything still on the old Wi-Fi isn't protected.

To avoid typing the password on every device, the app can show a QR code: Devices → Wi-Fi Networks, tap your network, then point the new device's camera at the code.

New devices are filtered by default. Until you assign a device to someone, social, gaming, streaming and adult sites stay blocked on it. That's deliberate — nothing slips through while you're still setting up.

That's the setup done.

Something not working?

  • No internet after plugging in? Check the cable is in the port marked WAN, and that your existing modem still shows online.
  • App won't pair? Restart the router and try again. If it still won't, hold a paperclip in the reset hole for 30 seconds — leave the power connected — and start over.
