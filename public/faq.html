Schedules & time limits

A schedule has days, a start and an end time, and what it covers: everything, Apple's categories like Games or Social, single apps, or websites. Then you pick the rule:

  • Block closes what you pick in these hours. For example, games and social during school.
  • Allow opens what you pick only in these hours. For example, games only from 5 to 6 PM.

Start from a ready-made suggestion for kids or teens and change it whenever you like. A kid can have up to 20 schedules.

When a schedule covers All apps and categories, every app on your kid's device closes except the ones you always allow. Phone is always allowed. Bedtime is the classic example.

The ones you put under Always allow on your kid's page: Messages, Maps, school apps, whatever your kid needs. They stay open even during Downtime.

Turn on Time limit in an Allow schedule and pick the minutes: from 15 minutes up to 12 hours, in 15-minute steps. For example, 30 minutes of games between 3 and 6 PM. When the time is used up, WhiteCat closes those apps until the next window. A kid can have up to 6 schedules with a time limit.

The app tells you when they overlap, when, and which one wins there, so you never have to guess.

Plan & billing

Your first month is free. Then $0.99 a month or $9.99 a year, for the whole family: every kid, every iPhone and iPad, both parents. WhiteCat Kids is always free.

The parent who created the family starts the plan in WhiteCat Parental Control. The second parent doesn't pay again.

On your iPhone, go to Settings → your name → Subscriptions → WhiteCat, or tap Manage subscription in WhiteCat's Settings. Cancel at least 24 hours before the plan renews; you keep it until the end of the period you paid for.

Apple handles payments and refunds for the App Store. Ask for one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Open WhiteCat and tap Restore purchases on the plan screen. Still stuck? Email us at support@whitecatcloud.com.

Your account

In Settings → Parents, tap Invite a parent. On their iPhone, they install WhiteCat, sign in, tap Join a family with a code and type the code you see. The code works while that screen is open. They then see the kids, change schedules and answer asks too.

Your kids' schedules keep running on their devices. Sign in with the same Apple Account to pick up where you left off.

In Settings, tap Delete account. It deletes your family, your kids' schedules and asks, and their devices' link to WhiteCat, so the devices stop following any schedule. It can't be undone. If another parent is in your family, remove them first. Want us to do it? See Delete your account.
