Accessibility Statement

Last reviewed: 2026-06-09

Conformance target

We aim to meet Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 at Level AA, consistent with the European Accessibility Act (Directive (EU) 2019/882) effective 28 June 2025.

Current status

We perform regular reviews and address barriers as they're identified. Known limitations:

  • Hero animations may not fully respect prefers-reduced-motion on all browsers — being addressed.
  • Some marketing-page images use decorative alt text by design; descriptions for informational images are being reviewed.

Compatibility

The site is tested with current versions of Chrome, Firefox, Safari, and Edge, plus the built-in screen readers VoiceOver (macOS, iOS), NVDA, and TalkBack (Android).

Feedback and complaints

If you encounter an accessibility barrier, please email accessibility@whitecatfamily.com. We respond within 5 working days.

If you're not satisfied with our response, you may contact the relevant supervisory authority in your country of residence.
