Set up in three steps

Two apps: WhiteCat Parental Control on your iPhone, and the free WhiteCat Kids on each kid's iPhone or iPad.

1

Install WhiteCat on your iPhone

Sign in with Apple, name your family and add your kids.

2

Install WhiteCat Kids on your kid's iPhone or iPad

Scan the code from your phone and allow Screen Time. Your kid needs a child account in your Apple Family Sharing group, on iOS or iPadOS 26.5 or later.

3

Pick a schedule. Done.

Night, School, Game time, Weekend: start with a ready-made one and change it whenever you like.
